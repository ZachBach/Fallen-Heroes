Permanent collection · Room 01

Вічна пам'ять

Eternal memory to the fallen defenders of Ukraine — the men and women who stood.

A permanent, non-commercial memorial. Every photograph, name, and object in these rooms is placed at the request of a family, a unit, or a community — and removed at their request.

Room 02 — Hall of light

One flame for each name we have been given.

The hall is rendered live — a floor of stone, a field of candles, and the embers that rise from them. Nothing here is a photograph of a real place. It is a room built to be quiet in.

Room 05 — Objects

What they carried.

Objects are shown on a plain field, lit flat, at true scale where scale is known. Conservation notes travel with each accession and are shown in full on request.

They were somebody's daughter, somebody's son, somebody's whole world. This gallery keeps the smallest facts about them, because the smallest facts are what a person actually is.

Curatorial statement · founding dedication
Room 06 — Accession

Adding a name, a face, or an object.

Families and units hold the record. The gallery holds the room. Zach Auerbach curates it personally — he reads every submission, confirms the documentation, and writes nothing the family has not approved.
